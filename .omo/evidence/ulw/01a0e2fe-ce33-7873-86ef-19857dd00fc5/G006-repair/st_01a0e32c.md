| N2 (D2) | Upload response computed `status`/`chunkCount` from extracted text (`document.chunks`), not written `rows` — a degrade upload lied INDEXED/N. | Response status derives from `rows` via `referenceDocumentStatus()`: `INDEXED` (rows>0) / `STORED_INDEX_PENDING` (text extracted, zero rows indexed) / `EMPTY` (no extractable text). `chunkCount` reports indexed chunks. ON CONFLICT now also refreshes `source_revision` — previously a re-upload left the stale revision recorded. |
| N3 (D3) | Dead `EMBEDDING_UNAVAILABLE` rejection reason in schema, HTTP status map (503), and internal union — no producer. | Removed from `ReferenceDocumentRejectionReasonSchema`, `REFERENCE_REJECTION_STATUS`, and `ReferenceRejectionReason`. No `ReferenceDocumentIndexState` type exists in the tree; the dead enum member was the only such artifact. |
